What the model learns

The Random Forest regressor uses distance, travel mode, transfers, departure hour and a simulated weather/traffic factor to estimate potential delay minutes.

Training Generated synthetic examples are split into training and test sets.
Evaluation MAE estimates average absolute error on held-out synthetic test data.
Limitations This is a learning demonstration, not a real-world delay forecast. No real operator history or live weather data is used.
